🧪 Sensors Used in Space Bot

🌡️ DHT11 - Temperature & Humidity

Issue: Spacecraft must maintain safe temperature and humidity levels.

Solution: DHT11 monitors both and triggers alerts if environment becomes uncomfortable or unsafe.

🧪 MQ-135 - Air Quality Sensor

Issue: Harmful gases like ammonia, benzene, CO₂ can build up inside spacecraft.

Solution: MQ-135 detects gas levels and ensures breathable, clean air.

🛢️ MQ-8 - Hydrogen Gas Sensor

Issue: Hydrogen leaks in spacecraft can be flammable and dangerous.

Solution: MQ-8 detects hydrogen gas to prevent ignition or toxicity risk.

📏 Ultrasonic Sensor (HC-SR04)

Issue: Navigation and object avoidance needed inside tight space stations.

Solution: Ultrasonic sensor maps distances for safe movements.

🩺 Pulse & Heartbeat Sensor

Issue: Monitoring astronaut health in real-time is crucial.

Solution: Pulse sensor tracks beats/min and shows alerts for abnormal values.
