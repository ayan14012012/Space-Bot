🛠️ Troubleshooting Guide

🔧 Common Issues & Quick Fixes

❌ DHT11 not updating temperature/humidity?

✅ Check VCC (3.3V/5V), GND, and data pin. Add 10K pull-up resistor. Avoid long wires.

❌ MQ-8 gas sensor reading zero?

✅ Allow sensor to preheat (1–3 mins). Use analog pin and confirm 5V input. Avoid tight enclosures.

❌ MQ-135 gas values unstable?

✅ Keep away from airflow or sunlight. Stabilize sensor in ambient air for 20s before reading.

❌ Ultrasonic sensor gives 0 or 999?

✅ Verify TRIG and ECHO pins are wired properly. Check power and ensure no close/reflective objects.

❌ Pulse sensor reads 0 or NaN?

✅ Clean sensor, ensure finger placement. Read from A0. Delay at least 5s after boot for accuracy.

❌ No data being sent to web server?

✅ Ensure WiFi SSID/password are correct. Server IP must be reachable. Use Serial Monitor to debug.
