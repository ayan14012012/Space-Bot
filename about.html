🌌 About Space Bot

🚀 Project Overview

Space Bot is a futuristic environmental monitoring system designed to help astronauts maintain a safe and livable habitat during space missions. It constantly tracks air quality, temperature, humidity, radiation, pressure, and other vital conditions.

🎯 Purpose

Our goal is to provide real-time alerts and emergency support to astronauts through an offline-first interface, ensuring safety and timely action in harsh space environments.

👨‍🚀 Target Users

Astronauts, research teams, space agencies, and students working on simulation-based space exploration and safety systems.
