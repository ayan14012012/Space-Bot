🚨 Emergency Protocols

📡 Communication Failure

Lost communication with base station.

Check antenna alignment, reboot amplifier, or switch to backup ESP radio.

🧪 Gas Leak (via MQ8)

Hydrogen or toxic gases detected at high levels.

Seal airlocks, wear oxygen mask, start air purification system.

🌡️ Temperature/Humidity Alert (via DHT11)

Temperature > 30°C or Humidity > 75%.

Check HVAC system, activate cooling manually, monitor crew vitals.

💓 Health Warning (via Pulse Sensor)

Astronaut pulse abnormal or not detected (NaN).

Reposition sensor, check connections, allow rest and hydration.

💧 Sensor Failure (Any)

DHT11 not updating / Pulse value NaN.

Verify wiring, sensor power, restart capsule microcontroller (Wemos/UNO).
